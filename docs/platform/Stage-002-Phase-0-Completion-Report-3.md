## 2. C8 — Measurable unknowns: **3 of 4 RESOLVED from source**

This is the most valuable output of this continuation, and two results change accepted classifications.

### Unknown 2 — `BinLocation` / `BinStock`: **OPERATIONAL, not dormant. Resolved.**

*Evidence:* **186 references**. Decisively, `BinLocationId` is a **first-class field on `StockService`'s own movement
contracts** — `BinLocationId` (destination), `SourceBinLocationId` (pick source), and a bin on opening stock. Plus
`GetBinStocksAsync` / `InitializeBinStockFromDefaultsAsync` on the writer's interface, consumers in
`ProcurementService`, `SellingService`, `WarehouseService`, `InventoryController`, **5 views**, and **13 write
operations**.

**Bins already participate in real stock movements through the stock writer.**

**Classification confirmed: Stage 4 = EXTEND AND REDESIGN** over an operational foundation — *not* "adopt and
activate", and certainly not a Full Platform Build. Recorded as RISK-019, re-worded from "underused" to
"operational and underused".

### Unknown 3 — `UoMConversion`: **OPERATIONAL. Resolved.**

*Evidence:* read by **`StockService:236`** (the stock writer) and **`PosOrderService:547`** (the live POS selling
path), plus `ItemService`, `InventoryApiController`, `InventoryController` — and notably
**`IntegrityCheckService` already validates conversions**.

Conversions are live in stock, POS and API paths. **2C must preserve every existing conversion read**; Unit Sets are
additive on top. Recorded as RISK-021.

### Unknown 4 — `ItemComponent` / `CompositeType`: **LOAD-BEARING. Resolved — and this is a new risk.**

*Evidence:* consumed by **`StockService:319` and `StockService:1189` (kit explosion inside the stock writer)**,
`PricingService:311` (determines whether an item is "manufactured"), **`PosOrderService:302` and `:1746`** (component
explosion at sale, semi-finished handling), `ManufService`, `PosSetupService`, `InventoryApiController`,
`PosAppController`. 22 `CompositeType` and 12 `ProductionMethod` references.

**Composite items are load-bearing in the two-writer core and in the POS selling path.** This dependency was
undocumented. Any composite redesign touches the stock writer, pricing, POS and manufacturing simultaneously —
which makes the nine-invariant stock-valuation gate mandatory rather than precautionary.

**New: RISK-033 (High).**

### Unknown 1 — Variants-as-separate-items: **BLOCKED.**

*Missing:* how many item groups represent size/colour/material variants, and whether conversion can be inferred safely.
*Reason:* it requires **data**, and the brief permits a read-only production assessment **only with explicit
approval**, which has not been given. Source alone cannot answer it — there is no variant model to inspect.
*Impact:* **2C cannot be sized.** This is the single largest unknown in Stage 2 scope.
*Next action:* request approval for a **read-only** measurement (candidate groups by shared category + name prefix +
price proximity), or supply an anonymised extract. **No automatic merging under any circumstances.**
Recorded as **RISK-034**.
