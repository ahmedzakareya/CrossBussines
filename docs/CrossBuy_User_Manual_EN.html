0Overview & system map

Before you start: every module talks to Accounting automatically. You never hand-key entries for daily operations — the system writes them balanced and guards their invariants.

Module map

Human Resourcesemployees · payroll · leave
→
Inventoryitems · movements · cost
→
Sales & Purchasingquotes · orders · invoices
→
Accountingjournal engine · GL

Each arrow means “feeds entries into.” Any operation (payroll run, goods receipt, sales invoice) reaches Accounting as a balanced entry.

Everything is connected

Inventory, payroll and sales post their own entries via one engine (JournalEntryService). No manual entries for these.

Invariants never break

Inventory value = the inventory GL account · customer/vendor controls = their subledgers · the trial balance always balances. A daily integrity check guards them.

Two languages

Every screen is Arabic/English with correct alignment (RTL/LTR). Switch language from the menu footer or header.

Roles & governance

Inventory and accounting roles, segregation of duties, and approval thresholds for large amounts. Open until the first role is assigned.

Confirm, then notify

Every edit, save, approve, reject, delete or post first shows a branded confirmation dialog (green for approve/save, red for reject/delete). Nothing runs until you confirm — and after it runs, a toast notification reports success or failure.

1Sign-in & navigation

Sign in

  1. Open the system, enter your username and password, and click “Sign in.”
  2. You land in your portal by role: the back-office (admin staff) or the employee self-service portal.

Side menu

Each module has its own menu, organized into collapsible categories. Click a category to open it; the active item is highlighted. Use “Search menu” to jump to any screen.

Open any recordEvery table has a direct “Open” 👁 button on its row that opens the record’s card/detail immediately.
Employee portalShows only the employee’s own links: profile, leaves, payslips and attendance — not the admin menu.

2Human Resources

From the employee file to the monthly payslip and final settlement — with leave, attendance and documents in between.

Employees, structure & documents

  1. “Employees”: add the employee and their personal/job data (title, branch, department, joining date) and photo.
  2. “Org structure”: build the tree Company ← Branch ← Department ← Employee. It drives the approval manager and the cost center automatically.
  3. “Contracts & documents”: record contracts (start/end dates) and classified documents (ID/passport/qualification/insurance) with expiry dates. “Expiry alerts” shows what’s due and can notify owners.

Pick the employee and termination date; the system computes service years, leave value and a suggested end-of-service gratuity. Posting creates a balanced entry (Dr settlement expenses / Cr bank), deactivates the employee and closes the contract automatically.

3Accounting & finance

The general ledger, the customer/vendor/bank cycles and the reports — all on one journal engine.

Essentials

  • Chart of accounts: create/edit accounts; a collapsible tree with search. Control accounts aren’t posted to manually.
  • Journals: a balanced manual entry (for non-automated ops); the system rejects unbalanced or closed-period entries.
  • Cost centers & fiscal periods: centers from the org tree, periods opened/closed to block posting by date.

Banks, assets, tax & closing

  • Banks & cash: bank accounts and cash boxes, transfers between them, and bank reconciliation against the statement.
  • Fixed assets: categories, assets, periodic depreciation and disposal. Buying an “Asset”-type item auto-capitalizes it.
  • Tax: tax codes, periodic VAT returns, and e-invoice (ETA) status.
  • Statements & closing: trial balance and financial statements, plus a year-end close that moves profit to retained earnings.

4Inventory & supply

Master data

  • “Categories”: map each category’s accounts (inventory, COGS, adjustment, GRNI) — items inherit them.
  • “Items”: code/barcode/UoM/type, multiple units and conversions, and composite (Kit) items. The item card shows analytics: last operation/purchase, average purchase & cost, last in/out dates, and a balance-over-time chart.

6Governance & invariants

Approvals & segregation of duties

→
Another approvercreator ≠ approver

  • Accounting roles: Chief Accountant / Accountant / Cashier / Auditor — actions gated by role.
  • Inventory roles: Warehouse keeper (their branch) / Purchasing / Inventory manager / Auditor.
  • Approval threshold: large journals/payments require the Chief Accountant; a creator can’t approve their own entry.

Period lock & integrity guard

  • Any movement dated in a closed period is rejected with a clear message.
  • An integrity reconciliation (and a daily check) verifies all invariants and alerts the owner before any drift accumulates.

7Opening balances & go-live

  1. Set the cut-off date, then enter: stock (item×warehouse×qty×cost), customer & vendor balances, fixed assets (cost and accumulated depreciation), and the remaining trial-balance accounts.
  2. All balances offset against an “Opening balance equity” clearing account (3301) that must net to zero.
  3. “Verify” checks: stock=1103, customer/vendor controls=subledgers, trial balance balanced, and 3301=0. Then “Finalize” locks entry.
After finalizeThe system runs daily operations on top of these balances, with invariants guarded from day one.

8Reports & insights

  • Financial reports: trial balance, income statement, balance sheet, aging.
  • Inventory reports: valuation, slow-moving items, shortages, batches/expiry.
  • AI insights: journal anomaly detection, cash-flow forecast, and inventory analysis — surfaced as suggestions to review.

📖Glossary

  • GRNI: goods received whose invoice hasn’t arrived yet.
  • FEFO: issue the nearest-expiry stock first.
  • Moving average / FIFO: the two inventory costing methods.
  • Segregation of duties (SoD): the document creator can’t approve it.
  • Cost center: an organizational unit expenses are attributed to.
