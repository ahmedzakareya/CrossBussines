# 19 — Tablet UX

Tablet is a distinct work posture: touch-first, often landscape, sometimes shared or field-based. It is not “large mobile.”

Use two-column list/detail where it reduces navigation, collapsible Metronic aside/drawer, 44px controls, and restrained sticky actions. On-screen keyboard appearance must not hide active fields or confirmation. Dense tables may retain priority columns in landscape and transform in portrait.

Support pointer, touch and hardware keyboard without mode assumptions. POS and field/construction workflows may use specialized operational layouts. Test 768px portrait and 1024px landscape, RTL, split-screen narrowing, rotation, disconnected/reconnected states, and permission changes. Do not rely on hover discovery.
