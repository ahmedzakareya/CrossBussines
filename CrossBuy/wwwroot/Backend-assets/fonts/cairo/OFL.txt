WHY THIS FILE IS HERE
  BL/Reporting/Fonts/Cairo-Variable.ttf is compiled into the assembly as a manifest resource and
  written into every report document as a base64 data: URI, so a report renders in Cairo on any
  machine without the font being installed there. The OFL permits that redistribution and requires
  the licence to travel with the font; this file is that requirement, not a formality.

  The font is redistributed unmodified and is not sold on its own. The reserved-name clause is not
  engaged: the family is shipped under its own name, "Cairo", with no derivative naming.
